# Business Partner (BP) Creation

## Definition

A Business Partner (BP) in SAP is a central master record used to maintain information about a business entity such as a supplier or customer.

## Transaction Code

**T-Code:** `BP`

## Process Flow

**Business Partner Creation → Supplier Role Assignment → Vendor Master Data Maintenance → Purchase Process**

## Purpose

Business Partner Creation is used to create and maintain the supplier's central master data in SAP.

## Business Partner Creation Process

1. Execute transaction code `BP`.
2. Select the required Business Partner category.
3. Enter the Business Partner name and address details.
4. Maintain the required general information.
5. Create or assign the required supplier role.
6. Maintain purchasing-related information.
7. Maintain company code-related information.
8. Save the Business Partner.

## Key Information

- Business Partner Number
- Business Partner Name
- Address
- Search Terms
- Country
- Region
- Postal Code
- Supplier Role
- Company Code Data
- Purchasing Organization Data
